About Us

Built for accountable engineering delivery.

New Gate Engineering was founded by overseas experienced engineers and interior renovation specialists. The company later formed as New Gate Engineering Company Limited to provide integrated construction and renovation services for clients across Myanmar.

The team is supported by professional engineers and YCDC licensed senior engineers, and also serves as a local service provider and distributor for selected international instrument partners.
